Privacy Policy

ColorPals Studio: Kids Paint

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Nibin Chamayil ("we") handles information in ColorPals Studio: Kids Paint ("the App"). The App is designed for children and families.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, or store personal information on our own servers. The App has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

The App saves information on your device so it can work. This may include an optional age range, favorite categories, colored pictures and thumbnails, stars and badges, custom colors, and app settings such as sound preferences. This information remains in the App's local storage and is not sent to us. Deleting the App removes its locally stored information.

Purchases

Optional Brainy Pals Plus is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes payments through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card or billing details. The App receives purchase and entitlement information from Apple to unlock the purchase and support restoration and Family Sharing. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing and printing

Sharing or printing a picture is initiated by a parent or guardian through the device's system share or print interface. The picture is passed to the destination you choose. We do not receive or retain it. The destination's own privacy terms apply.

Children and parental controls

The App does not ask children for personal information. Purchases, parent settings, sharing, and printing are protected by a grown-ups check. Parents or guardians should supervise use of those features.

Third-party services

The App uses Apple's operating-system and App Store services for features such as purchases, sharing, printing, and speech. We do not add third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Those Apple services are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's practices change. The effective date at the top indicates when it was last revised.

Contact
